## 1. Design Rules & Visual Strategy

1. **Rule of 3 Elements**: A human subject (Hardik with authentic emotion) + High-contrast focal point (Phone screen / Bank alert) + Minimalist curiosity text (max 3 words).
2. **Hardik's Character Consistency**: Maintain exact facial structure, curly-wavy black hair, connected beard, and lean-to-average build. No fake CGI plastic skin or artificial over-beautification.
3. **Contrast & Mobile Legibility**: Visuals must pop on a tiny 2-inch mobile screen in a crowded YouTube feed. Dark moody tech backgrounds with saturated punchy accents (crimson red warning or neon cyan).

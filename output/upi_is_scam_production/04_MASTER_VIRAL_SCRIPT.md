"Lekin jab tak yeh systems fully mature nahi hote, as a tech builder main aapko **4 golden rules** de raha hoon jo aapko aaj hi apply karne hain:

Kabhi bhi apne main salary account ya life-savings emergency fund account ko Google Pay ya PhonePe se connect mat kijiye. Yeh fundamental rule apne parents aur relatives ke dimaag mein baithaiye: **UPI PIN sirf paise bhejne ke liye daala jaata hai, paise receive karne ke liye kabhi nahi.** Agar koi QR code scan karne bol raha hai paise dene ke liye, it is a 100% scam.

**Rule #4: If Frozen, Act Within 24 Hours.**  
Agar aapka account bina galti ke freeze hota hai, toh branch manager se specific **Cyber Cell Crime Number aur Police Station** ki details mangiye.

## Script Breakdown

### [00:00 – 00:08] HOOK
**Hardik (Host):**
"Imagine kariye: subah aap apne regular cafe ya kirana store pe jaate hain. ₹20 ki chai ke liye phone nikaalte hain, QR scan karte hain, aur payment instantly Success ho jaata hai. Lekin theek char ghante baad aapka poora bank account freeze ho jaata hai."

### [00:08 – 00:25] PROBLEM & CONTEXT
**Hardik (Voiceover / B-Roll / Evidence):**
"Credit card mein fraud hone par transaction reverse ho sakta hai, lekin UPI ko banaya gaya tha irreversible teen second settlement ke liye. Aur scam syndicates ne is 3-second speed ko ek lethal weapon bana liya Digital Arrest aur fake police video calls ke zariye."

### [00:25 – 00:50] EXPLANATION (THE ECONOMIC PARADOX)
**Hardik (Host / Interactive Data Graphic):**
"Lekin banks fraud detection mein itna piche kyun hain? The answer is an economic paradox. Ek physical sau rupaye ke note ko print karne ka cost RBI ko lagta hai sirf ek rupaya ikyavan paise. Yeh note teen saal mein teen sau baar exchange hota hai, toh har transaction ka cost padta hai sirf aadha paisa. Lekin teen sau digital UPI transactions ka server cost padta hai poore 600 rupaye!"

### [00:50 – 00:75] EVIDENCE & SUBSIDY COLLAPSE
**Hardik (Voiceover / Chart / Document):**
"Zero-MDR rule ki wajah se banks legally merchant fee nahi le sakte. Pehle government subsidy deti thi 3,500 crore, fir 2,000 crore, aur ab subsidy girkar reh gayi hai sirf 427 crore. Banks ke paas fraud rokne ke liye budget hi nahi bacha!"

### [00:75 – 00:95] SURPRISE & REVEAL (THE 1930 DILEMMA & DUOPOLY)
**Hardik (Voiceover / Alert / Diagram):**
"Isi budget starvation ki wajah se jab helpline 1930 pe complaint aati hai, banks legal rule follow karne ke bajaye—jismein sirf disputed 500 rupaye pe lien lagna chahiye—poora bank account freeze kar dete hain! Upar se 80% market sirf do foreign-funded apps control karti hain jo har scan ke saath aapka GPS location aur social graph monitor kar rahi hain."

### [00:95 – 01:35] CONCLUSION & SURVIVAL RULES
**Hardik (Host / Closing Card):**
"Supreme Court ne ab temporary debit hold ka SOP diya hai aur RBI ne MuleHunter AI deploy kiya hai. Lekin apne aap ko bachaane ke liye do golden rules yaad rakhein: Salary account ke bajaye hamesha secondary buffer account UPI se link karein, aur yaad rakhein UPI PIN sirf paise BHEJNE ke liye hota hai, receive karne ke liye nahi. Stay alert, and stay safe."

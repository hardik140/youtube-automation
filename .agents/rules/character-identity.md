Whenever generating video scenes, thumbnail concepts, frame prompts (Nano Banana / Veo 3 / Imagen), or visual breakdowns featuring the channel's host/explainer, apply the following strict identity rules:

1. **Identity is Immutable**: Facial structure, hair, eyes, skin tone, beard pattern, height (173–175 cm), and athletic build must stay 100% constant across every image and video scene.
2. **Key Physical Features**:
   - Indian male, early 20s.
   - Warm medium-brown skin with real skin pores/texture (no beauty filter).
   - Thick black curly/wavy hair with volume on top, short-to-medium sides.
   - Connected natural short black beard and moustache.
   - Deep-set dark brown eyes, thick dark eyebrows, oval-oblong jawline.
3. **Role**: The main narrator, investigator, and builder who directly explains topics, uncovers systems, breaks down cases, and talks to the audience.
4. **Prompt Lock Snippet**:
   `Young Indian male in his early 20s, warm medium-brown skin, oval-oblong face, defined natural jawline, thick dark straight eyebrows, dark brown deep-set eyes with calm confident expression, thick dense curly-wavy black hair with textured volume on top and short sides, naturally dense connected short black beard and moustache, lean-to-average athletic build, moderately broad shoulders. Photographed on 50mm-85mm portrait lens, natural realistic lighting.`
5. **Negative Rules**: Avoid generic CGI faces, beauty filters, clean-shaven faces, flat straight hair, exaggerated bodybuilder builds, or inconsistent facial bone structure.
